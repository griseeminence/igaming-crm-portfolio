# iGaming CRM questions and my position

### 1. Where to start reactivation: bonus or content?
Content first, then the cheapest offer that works, and the most generous one only for players whose return is worth it.

**Why:** part of the effect comes from the contact itself, and that part is free. A bonus for a player who would have come back for a game recommendation is money thrown away.

### 2. Are holdout groups worth the revenue they cost?
Yes, almost always. The size depends on the audience: small for big audiences, bigger for small audiences or rare events.

**Why:** a campaign without a holdout always looks better than it is, because some players would have done the same thing anyway.

### 3. Should VIPs get more bonuses?
VIPs get more service and attention, but not automatically more money. Any monetary reward goes through a test.

**Why:** a host who knows the player is worth more than another percent of cashback.

### 4. Main KPI: response or incremental effect?
Incremental effect, in money (incremental NGR after costs). Response stays as "diagnostics".

**Why:** a campaign can have a great response and almost zero result, because most responders would have done the same without it.

### 5. Optimise for retention or revenue?
Revenue (NGR). Retention bought with bonuses can lose money.

**Why:** retention can be bought. If keeping a player costs more than they bring in, that's not a success.

### 6. The more communication the better?
Fewer messages, but better content and campaigns. With a shared cap and a clear priority when several fire at once.

**Why:** an unsubscribe removes the player from all future campaigns, and that cost never shows up in the weekly report.

### 7. Segmentation: simple rules or predictive models?
Both. Rules for what people work with every day, models for ranking and prioritising.

**Why:** a model nobody trusts is a model nobody uses.

### 8. Big welcome bonus or a better first experience?
A moderate welcome bonus, and the money goes into onboarding quality: verification, payments, tips in the first session.

**Why:** a failed payment or a confusing lobby makes a player leave, however big the bonus is.

### 9. Is SMS still worth paying for?
Keep it for the moments that matter and the players who matter: the last step of a valuable win-back, an urgent service message.

**Why:** if you send SMS everywhere, it turns into expensive noise.

### 10. Is cross-sell always good?
Cross-sell to players with some propensity for the other product, judge it on total value across all products, and leave players with risk markers alone.

**Why:** if casino revenue went up by exactly as much as sports revenue went down, we gained nothing.

### 11. Attribution: last touch or holdout?
Holdouts for decisions, attribution only for operational reporting.

**Why:** a player who would have deposited anyway will still click the last email. Attribution will count that as a result.

### 12. Should we let lapsed players go?
After a reasonable win-back window, move them to a list with rare sends, and over time stop writing to them.

**Why:** a clean base protects inbox placement, which active players need.
