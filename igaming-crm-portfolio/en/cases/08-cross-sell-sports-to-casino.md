# Case 08 - Cross-selling sports bettors into casino

> **In short:** I cross-sell only to players who already show some interest in casino, and only when they're not busy with sports. First content about one product. I judge it on the incremental NGR of both products together, so we don't end up just moving money from one pocket to another.

## Situation
*Made-up numbers for the exercise.*

- 40% of active players bet on sports only.
- Management wants more of them to try casino: multi-product players look more valuable.
- What happens now: a "Try casino - get 50 free spins" banner for all sports players.

## How I approach it

"Multi-product players are more valuable" is a correlation. People who already like both products are probably more engaged in general. It doesn't follow that pushing casino on a pure bettor will make them more valuable. The question is whether the cross-sell creates **extra** value or just moves money from sports to casino.

## Segmentation
I wouldn't treat "sports players" as one group:

| Segment | Likely response |
|---|---|
| Already visited the casino (demo, one session) | highest interest, I start with them |
| Bet on sports often | engaged, fast games might work for them |
| Bet only at weekends, around events | low interest, they come for the football |
| High-value sports players | careful: breaking their habits can hurt core revenue |

## Journey
| When | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A quiet moment (no big matches, after the weekend) | In-app | Casino intro tile: games close to sports interests (live shows, fast games) |
| +2 days | Email | "New to casino?" How it works, demo games, how to set limits |
| +5 days | Push | Casino free spins, separate from any sports offers, with simple terms |
| Day 14 | | Exit |

## Exclusions
- Players who already play casino.
- Players who opted out of casino marketing; jurisdictions without casino.
- Bonus abuse flags.

## How I would measure it
- **Primary KPI:** first casino bet within 14 days.
- **Decision metric:** **total** NGR per player (sports + casino) over 60 days, test vs control. If casino NGR went up and sports NGR went down by the same amount, the campaign created nothing.
- **Holdout:** 20%. The baseline conversion is low, so the control group needs to be bigger.
